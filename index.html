There is no single way through pet loss

Grief can begin before a goodbye and continue long after it. Choose the stage that feels closest to where you are today. You can return, move between stages, or explore at your own pace.

A familiar image of hope

The Rainbow Bridge

The Rainbow Bridge is a comforting idea shared by many people after the death of a pet. It imagines a peaceful meadow where animals are healthy and whole again, waiting until they can be reunited with the people who loved them.

For some, the image offers reassurance that love and connection continue beyond death. For others, it may simply express a hope that a beloved pet is safe, free from pain, and surrounded by peace. You are invited to hold the story in whatever way fits your own beliefs and relationship with your pet.

The poem is credited to Scottish writer Edna Clyne-Rekhy, who wrote it in 1959 after the death of her Labrador, Major.
